# 02 - Mentorship or discipleship

**What to build:** A Ministry's texts read *You have been paired for mentorship* or *You have been paired for discipleship*, by that Ministry's own choice.

**Blocked by:** James. Parked by him on 2026-09-21: *leave it at discipleship for this week, but we're going to change this to discipleship/mentorship.*

**Status:** needs-info

## Decided by James, 2026-09-21

> Choosing between mentorship and discipleship determines whether the 1:2 and 1:1 appear as "you have been paired for mentorship" or "you have been paired for discipleship."
> That's what the differentiator is.

> Eventually, the discipleship/mentor question on the Ministry settings page will actually be used for the text, whether it says mentorship or discipleship.

## What is true today

- Every Ministry's Starter Messages say *discipleship*, including a Ministry whose own words are *mentor* and *mentee*.
- **There is no discipleship-or-mentorship question on Ministry settings.**
  The page asks two words a Ministry types freely: what it calls a person leading, and what it calls a person being discipled.
  A Ministry can type *shepherd* and *friend*, and no program turns *shepherd* into a word like mentorship.
- The word for a person leading is used in one text, the invitation: *you've been paired with someone to be their [mentor]*.
- The word for a person being discipled is used in no text since 2026-09-21. James chose to keep its field as it is, doing nothing for now.

## For James, when he takes this up

1. **How a Ministry says which it is.**
   Put to him with three options on 2026-09-21, and he chose *not now* without choosing between them.
   *Recommended:* a choice of two on Ministry settings, *Discipleship* or *Mentorship*, beside the two words a Ministry already types, so a Ministry keeps its own words for people.
   The others: a third word typed freely; or one choice that replaces the two typed words.
2. **Which texts follow it.** Both Starter Messages, by his words. Whether the invitation text does is not said.
3. **Whether a group reads differently.** He named *the 1:2 and 1:1*; a group of five may be meant to read the same or not.
4. Any of these is one new column, filled in for Ministries that already exist: *mentorship* where their word for a leader is *mentor*, *discipleship* otherwise.
