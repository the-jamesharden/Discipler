# Discipler — The Pastor Experience *(Churches Using Planning Center)*

*Everything in the standard pastor experience still applies. This page covers what changes when a church connects Planning Center.*

## 1. Signing Up

Same two doors: **Free for the first 14 days**, or **book a demo** for a guided setup.

The difference comes at account creation — the pastor can choose **Sign in with Planning Center** instead of creating a Discipler password. Planning Center already knows who's on staff and at what permission level, so admin access follows the church's existing structure. Email login stays available too, so a Planning Center outage never locks anyone out of their own dashboard.

## 2. Connecting Planning Center

One click from settings authorizes Discipler with read access to **People only** — never Giving, Services, or Check-Ins. The pastor sees exactly what's being shared before approving.

## 3. Building the Ministry — From the Roster They Already Have

Instead of typing 40 email addresses, the pastor browses their actual church roster inside Discipler.

- **Start from a List.** Discipler shows the church's existing Planning Center Lists — "Small Group Leaders," "Fall Membership Class," "College Students." Pick one and the roster narrows to those people. The church already did this segmentation work; Discipler just reads it.
- **Filter by tag.** Tags narrow the view further. Tags are only ever a filter — Discipler never decides someone should be a mentor because of a label. The pastor always chooses.
- **Invite in bulk.** Select people individually or multi-select a whole group, then choose **Invite as Mentor** or **Invite as Mentee**. Everyone selected receives the invitation email immediately.

People already enrolled in Discipler are hidden automatically, so nobody gets invited twice — even across repeat imports.

**Consent still happens on the form.** Imported people appear on the dashboard as **Invited**, not enrolled. Each one fills out the church's join form, where they consent to text messages and set their availability, exactly as they would otherwise. Planning Center saves the pastor's typing; it never speaks for the person.

## 4. Workflow Triggers *(Optional)*

For churches running assimilation through Planning Center Workflows, discipleship can become the automatic next step.

The pastor maps a workflow step to a Discipler invitation:

| When someone completes… | Discipler sends… |
|---|---|
| New Believer follow-up | Mentee invitation |
| Membership Class | Mentee invitation |
| Baptism follow-up | Mentee invitation |
| Leader onboarding | Mentor invitation |

Now the pipeline the church already runs feeds discipleship without anyone remembering to do it. Every triggered invitation appears in the dashboard's invite log with its source, and any mapping can be reviewed or switched off at any time.

These are still **invitations** — the person receives an email, fills out the form, and consents on their own. Nothing enrolls anyone automatically.

## 5. Pairing, Check-Ins, and Everything After

Identical to the standard experience: suggested pairs based on schedule overlap, pastor approves, both parties confirm by text, weekly check-ins, stall flags on the ministry's own thresholds, pause and opt-out statuses on the dashboard.

## 6. Writing Back to Planning Center

This is where Discipler stops being a separate tool. A **Discipler** tab appears on each person's Planning Center profile with fields Discipler keeps current:

- **Discipler Status** — Unpaired, Paired, Paused, Opted Out
- **Discipler Mentor** — who they're paired with
- **Discipler Last Check-In** — when they last reported meeting

A pastor searching Planning Center now sees discipleship state without opening Discipler at all. Contact information stays owned by Planning Center; discipleship data is owned by Discipler. Neither system overwrites the other's territory.

## 7. Staying in Sync

When a phone number changes in Planning Center, Discipler updates automatically. When someone is removed from Planning Center, their Discipler texts stop. The pastor maintains one roster, not two.

## 8. Nothing Depends on the Connection

Every integrated path has a manual equivalent. A pastor can invite, pair, and run their entire ministry with Planning Center disconnected. Discipler works better alongside Planning Center — it never requires it.
