# Discipler — The Mentee Experience

*From invitation to being discipled, end to end.*

## 1. Getting In — Two Pathways

- **Invited:** The mentee receives an email invitation from their pastor: *"You've been invited to join [Church]'s discipleship ministry,"* linking to the church's private form.
- **Inbound:** The mentee scans the church's QR code or enters the short link they were given.

Either way, they land on the same form — tokenized to their specific church — and submitting it places them directly into that church's ministry pool.

## 2. The Form

The mentee provides their name, phone number, and their weekly availability. Availability is a tap-to-select grid — days across the top, time blocks down the side — where they mark every window that could work. Selecting several makes it far easier to find a mentor whose schedule lines up. Two consent lines appear at the bottom:

> *"By submitting, you consent to receive text messages from the ministry."*
>
> *"Your phone number is shared with the ministry and with your paired mentor."*

The availability they enter here is what their future mentor will see when scheduling their weekly meetup.

## 3. Confirmation Text

Immediately on submitting, the mentee receives a welcome text from Discipler: they're in, their pastor will match them with a mentor soon, expected message frequency, and STOP/HELP instructions. They know from minute one what to expect.

## 4. Pairing

When the pastor pairs them with a mentor, the mentee receives:

> *"You have been paired with [Mentor] from [Church]. Do you agree to this pairing? Reply 1 for yes, 2 for no."*

- Both parties reply yes → the pair goes active. The mentor receives the mentee's contact info and reaches out to set the first meetup within the mentee's stated availability.
- If the mentee declines, that's private — they simply return to the pool, and the pastor follows up to find a better match. Declining never requires an awkward conversation.

## 5. The Weekly Rhythm

The mentee meets with their mentor weekly at their agreed time. By default, weekly check-in texts go to the mentor — the mentee just shows up and grows.

**If the pastor enables mentee check-ins,** the mentee also receives the weekly texts:

> *"Did you meet with [Mentor] this week? Reply 1 for yes, 2 for no."*
> *"How did the meeting go? Reply A for good, B for okay, C for concern."*
> *(If C:)* *"How can we be praying for you? What was the specific concern?"*
> *"Thank you. We appreciate your response."*

The mentee's responses are confidential — concerns route to the pastor for care, not to the mentor. This is the safe channel that makes honesty possible.

**If the mentor goes quiet:** the mentee is never stuck waiting and wondering. Missed meetings and unanswered check-ins surface on the pastor's dashboard automatically, so the pastor steps in — the mentee doesn't have to report anyone or fix it themselves.

## 6. Website Access

If the mentee wants to view ministry resources on the website, they log in with their phone number and a one-time code sent by text — no password created, nothing to remember, and no way for anyone else to access their information with just a name and church.

## 7. Life Happens

- **Updating availability:** schedules change — the mentee updates their available time slots from their login anytime, and their mentor sees the new times.
- **Reply PAUSE** to any Discipler text for a season that's too full. A confirmation comes back first — *"Pause your check-ins for 4 weeks? Reply YES to confirm"* — so a stray tap never changes anything. The pairing stays intact and check-ins resume automatically.
- **Use the dashboard.** Every check-in carries a link to a page where the mentee can pause, step out of the pairing, or leave the program. It requires a one-time code to log in, so it's always deliberate.
- **Reply HELP** anytime to see the options again, including how to pause and how to reach a pastor.
- **Stepping away:** leaving — for a season or for good — takes one action from the dashboard, and it shows up on the pastor's side simply as a status change. No permission needed, no explanation required, no stigma.
- **If STOP gets used by accident:** it ends all Discipler texts immediately and can't be undone from the pastor's side. But the pairing isn't deleted — texting **START** (that exact word) brings everything back, including the existing mentor pairing. The ministry's help page explains this in plain language.

**Every six weeks,** a rhythm check arrives: *"Is this still a good season for it? Reply 1 to keep going, 2 to talk with your pastor."* It's a built-in moment to reassess, so nothing has to be brought up out of the blue.

**Once a month,** the first check-in of the calendar month includes a short reminder — *"Reply STOP to opt out. Msg&data rates may apply."* Every other message stays clean and simple.
