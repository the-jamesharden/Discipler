Discipler helps churches and ministries run discipleship and mentorship relationships: pairing people, checking in by text each week, and showing ministry leaders where care is needed.
This policy explains what information Discipler handles, why, who can see it, and the choices you have.

Discipler is operated by Discipler LLC, a Georgia limited liability company ("Discipler," "we," "us").

## 1. Your ministry and Discipler

Most of the information in Discipler belongs to the church or ministry that invited you (your "ministry").
Your ministry decides who is added, who is paired with whom, and how the program is run.
Discipler stores and processes that information on your ministry's behalf, following its instructions, to provide the service.
In privacy-law terms, your ministry is the controller (or "business") and Discipler is its processor (or "service provider").

Your ministry may have its own privacy notice describing how it uses your information, and you can ask your ministry about it directly.

A smaller set of information Discipler collects for its own purposes, such as messages you send to our support address and visits to our website.
This policy covers both.

Each ministry's information is kept separate from every other ministry's.
One ministry can never see another's people, and we never combine information across ministries, even in anonymized or aggregate form.

## 2. What we collect

### Information you give on your ministry's intake form

- Your name and mobile phone number
- Your email address, if you choose to give one
- Your gender and age range (for example, 25-34), never your exact age or date of birth
- The times of the week you are usually available
- The discipleship goal you choose from your ministry's list, and whether you want to lead or be discipled
- The group you chose, if your ministry invited you to join a group
- Your answers to the consent questions: whether you agree to receive texts, and whether you agree to your phone number being shared with the people in your discipleship relationship.
  We record when you answered, which version of the wording you saw, and whether you reached the form from a link or a QR code.

### Information your ministry adds

- Names, phone numbers and email addresses your ministry's administrators add to its roster, for example by importing a spreadsheet
- The relationships your ministry forms, the discipleship materials it assigns, and the notes and decisions its administrators record while following up

Being added to a roster does not sign you up for anything.
Discipler texts nobody and pairs nobody until that person completes the intake form and gives consent themselves.

### Information from text messages

- Replies to weekly check-ins: whether a meeting happened, how it went, and anything a leader writes when they raise a concern
- Keywords such as STOP, START, HELP, PAUSE, RESUME and SWAP, and any other reply you send
- Delivery information for each text, such as when it was sent and whether it arrived

Only leaders are asked check-in questions.
If you are being discipled, the texts you receive are mostly to welcome you and introduce your leader.

### Account information

Leaders and ministry administrators sign in with their phone number and a password.
Your password is stored in hashed form, and nobody at Discipler or your ministry can read it.
We keep records of sign-ins and sessions so we can keep accounts secure.

### Materials

Ministry administrators may upload discipleship materials, such as PDFs or written text.

### Technical information

When you use the Discipler website or app, our hosting providers automatically record information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, the pages you request and the time of each request.
We use this only to run, secure and fix the service.

Discipler uses only the cookies needed to keep you signed in.
We do not use analytics cookies, advertising trackers or cross-site tracking.
Our public website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser tells Google your IP address when the page loads.

### Support

If you email us, we keep your message and our reply so we can help you.

### Sensitive information

Taking part in a church's discipleship program can reveal your religious beliefs.
Concerns written in a check-in reply may describe personal, family or spiritual matters.
Gender and age range are used for safeguarding when pairing.
We use this information only to provide the service to your ministry, never for any other purpose.

Please do not send health records, financial account numbers, Social Security numbers or other government ID numbers through Discipler.
The product does not need them.

## 3. How we use information

- To run your discipleship relationship: suggesting pairings to your ministry using gender, age range, availability and discipleship goal, and sending the texts that go with the relationship.
  A suggestion is only a suggestion.
  Every relationship is formed by a person at your ministry and accepted by its leader.
- To show your ministry's administrators how relationships are going, which ones may need care, and what needs follow-up.
- To prepare reports for your ministry about its own program, such as response rates, how meetings are going, how materials are working, and patterns by age range and gender within that ministry.
- To create and secure accounts, prevent fraud and abuse, and keep each ministry's information separate.
- To answer support requests.
- To fix problems and improve how Discipler works for your ministry.
- To bill ministries for the service and keep business records.
- To meet legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

We do not use your information to market to you.
We do not use artificial intelligence to make decisions about you, and we do not use your information to train artificial intelligence models.
If that ever changes, we will update this policy before it does.

## 4. Text messages

When you complete your ministry's intake form and check the text-message consent box, which is unchecked by default, you agree to receive text messages from your ministry through Discipler.
A sample of the form is at trydiscipler.com/sms-opt-in.

- Messages are sent by Discipler LLC on behalf of the church or ministry running your discipleship program, and carry your ministry's name.
  Some messages also name Discipler as the service delivering them.
- Messages may include a welcome message, an introduction to your leader or the people you will lead, meeting reminders, weekly check-ins, follow-up questions and program coordination.
- Message frequency varies.
  Leaders typically receive 3-5 messages per week during an active relationship (up to approximately 24 per month), and people being discipled receive far fewer.
- Message and data rates may apply.
- Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase.
  Discipler's check-ins run by text, so the intake form asks for this consent.
  If you do not want texts, talk to your ministry about being discipled without Discipler.
- Reply STOP at any time to stop all messages.
  Reply START to opt back in.
- Reply HELP for help, or email support@trydiscipler.com.
- Leaders can also reply PAUSE, RESUME or SWAP to manage a single relationship.
- Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except the providers that deliver the text messages.

## 5. Who can see your information

**Your ministry's administrators** can see the information your ministry holds about you, including your phone number and email, your intake answers, your relationships, and check-in replies and concerns.

**The people in your discipleship relationship** see your name.
If you are being discipled, your leader can see your phone number only if you agreed to contact sharing on the intake form.
Discipler never puts anyone's phone number in a text message.

**Discipler** staff and contractors access information only when needed to run, support, secure or fix the service, or when the law requires it.

**Service providers** process information for us only to provide the service, under contracts that limit their use of it:

| Provider | What it does | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Supabase | Database and sign-in | Canada |
| Vercel | Website and app hosting | United States |
| Twilio | Sending and receiving text messages | United States |
| Google Fonts | Font delivery for our public website | United States |

We will keep this list current here.
Ministries can email support@trydiscipler.com to be told about changes to it.

**For legal reasons.**
We may disclose information if we believe in good faith it is required by law or by a valid request from a court or government agency, or is necessary to protect the safety of any person, to investigate fraud or abuse of the service, or to protect the rights of Discipler, our users or the public.
Where the law allows, we will tell the affected ministry before we do.

**If Discipler is sold or merged.**
If Discipler is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of it.
We will notify ministries before information is transferred and becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

We never sell personal information, and we never share it for targeted advertising.
We never give one ministry's information to another ministry.

## 6. Where information is stored

Discipler's database is hosted in Canada.
Our hosting and text-message providers process information in the United States.
By using Discipler, you understand that your information will be processed in Canada and the United States.

## 7. How long we keep information

We keep information for as long as your ministry uses Discipler, because your ministry's records of its relationships depend on it.
Discipler keeps a dated history of what happened rather than overwriting it, so the record of a past week stays accurate.

If your ministry removes you from its roster, Discipler stops sending you messages, takes you off every list, and deletes your sign-in account.
Your past history stays in your ministry's records unless you ask for it to be deleted (see section 8).

We keep consent records and opt-out (STOP) records for as long as we may need them to honor your choice and show that messages were sent with consent, even after other information is deleted.

When a ministry stops using Discipler, it can ask for a copy of its information within 30 days.
We then delete the ministry's information within 90 days, except backups, which are overwritten on a rolling basis within 30 more days, and anything we must keep by law.

Our service providers keep their own operational logs, such as text-message delivery records, for the periods set out in their policies.

## 8. Your choices and rights

You can ask to:

- see the information Discipler holds about you
- correct information that is wrong
- delete your information
- receive a copy of your information in a common format
- withdraw a consent you gave, or object to a use of your information

To make a request, contact your ministry, or email us at support@trydiscipler.com with "Privacy request" in the subject line.
Because your ministry controls most of your information, we will usually involve your ministry in answering, and we may pass your request to it.

We will ask you to confirm your identity before acting, usually by confirming the phone number we hold for you.
Someone you authorize may make a request for you if they show us your written permission.
We will respond within 45 days, and tell you if we need more time.

When we delete your information, we remove or de-identify your personal details.
Counts and reports that do not identify you may remain in your ministry's records.
We keep the minimum needed to honor a STOP request or to meet a legal obligation.

You will not be treated differently for using any of these rights.
If we decline a request, we will tell you why, and you can reply to ask us to reconsider.
Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under your state's or province's privacy laws, and we will honor them as those laws require.

You can stop all text messages at any time by replying STOP.

## 9. Security

Each ministry's information is separated in the database itself, so a request made for one ministry cannot read or change another ministry's information.
Information travels over encrypted connections, passwords are stored in hashed form, and access by Discipler staff is limited to what their work requires.
No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee that information will never be accessed without permission.

If we learn of a security breach affecting your information, we will notify the affected ministries without undue delay, and notify affected people directly where the law requires it.

## 10. Children

Discipler is for adults.
The intake form's age ranges start at 18, and ministries must not enroll anyone under 18.
We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.
If you believe a child's information is in Discipler, contact the ministry or email support@trydiscipler.com, and we will work with the ministry to delete it.

## 11. Tracking and Do Not Track

Because Discipler does not use tracking technologies, there is nothing for a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal to turn off.

## 12. Links to other sites

Our website links to other sites, such as Calendly for booking a call.
Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for how they handle information.

## 13. Changes to this policy

For a significant change, we will notify ministries by email at least 30 days before it takes effect.
We will never use information already collected in a materially different way without the consent that change requires.
