# Follow-Up

Follow-Up is everything that needs you, in one list, so nothing depends on you spotting it somewhere else.
The number on the tab is how many things are waiting.

## What shows up here

- **Stalled**: a relationship with no answer for {{stalledSilent}}, or "we didn't meet" for {{stalledNotMeeting}} in a row.
  It says which, and for how long.
- **Concern**: a Discipler answered D.
  Its words are on the card, never on the list.
- **Pause expired**: a pause has run its course and the relationship hasn't resumed.
- **Swap requested**: a Discipler asked to be paired with somebody else.
  Nothing changes until you act.
- **Awaiting acceptance**: a Discipler hasn't accepted a new relationship after {{acceptanceEscalation}}.
- **Invitation declined**, **Invitation expired** and **Not their number**: something went wrong with an invitation.
- **Wants a group** and **Wants to join a group**: somebody from your groups form is waiting to be placed or admitted.
- **Texted a keyword**: a Disciple texted PAUSE, RESUME or SWAP, which only a Discipler can use, so it is passed to you.
- **Pair not made**: a pair from your spreadsheet couldn't be made by the pairing rules.

Nothing on Follow-Up clears itself, except a stall, which clears when its Discipler answers that they met.

## Working an item

Press an item to open its card.
Everything open on that relationship is there together, each with what you can do about it, and **What has happened** lists the story so far.

- **Reach out.** The card shows the phone numbers of everyone who agreed to share theirs, with Call and Text.
  Opening the card sends nothing; you reach out yourself.
- **Resolve** clears an item once you have dealt with it.
- **Resolve and clear this text** clears a concern and deletes its words for good.
  Your church never builds up a file of people's hardest weeks.
- **Resume relationship** lifts a pause that has run out.
- **End relationship** is at the foot of the card.
  You say how it ended and why, and everyone in it can be paired again.

## Reading a concern

A concern's words show on the card of the relationship it is about, and nowhere else.
They are never listed, exported or put in a report.
Opening the card records that you read them, and the card says who has read them and when.
