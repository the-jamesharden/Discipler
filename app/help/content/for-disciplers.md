# What your Disciplers and Disciples see

Your Disciplers have a simple page of their own, and your Disciples take part entirely by text.
Neither can see your dashboard.

## Your Disciplers

A Discipler's first text is an invitation, sent when you pair them.
Its link shows who they have been paired with and for which church, and asks them to choose a password and accept.
Possession of their phone is how they prove who they are, and the link works for {{invitationLifetime}}.

Once they have accepted, they sign in with their phone number and password to see **The relationships you lead**.
For each relationship it shows:

- when everyone said they are free, on one weekly grid, so they can find a time to meet,
- the material they are working through, and
- the name and number of everyone in it who agreed to share theirs.

Nothing more: no answers, no reports, and nothing they can change about anyone else.
Disciplers arrange meetings themselves; Discipler never schedules anything.

Each week Discipler texts them their [check-in](/help/check-ins).
They can also text a word to Discipler at any time:

- **PAUSE** pauses check-ins for one relationship, for {{pausePeriods}} weeks.
  They choose how long; {{defaultPause}} is the default.
- **RESUME** starts a paused relationship again straight away.
- **SWAP** asks you to pair them with somebody else.
  It shows on [Follow-Up](/help/follow-up) and changes nothing until you act.

A pause needs nobody's approval, and Disciples are not told about it.
When a pause runs out, nothing restarts on its own: it waits on Follow-Up for you to resume or end it.

If a Discipler forgets their password, an Admin resets it from their page on the Roster.

## Your Disciples

Disciples have no account and no page.
They hear from your church by text:

- a welcome as soon as they complete Intake,
- a text when their relationship starts, with their Discipler's name and number, so a new number calling them isn't a stranger, and
- a text with a link when the material they are working through changes.

They are never asked check-in questions.
If a Disciple texts back, they are told to contact your church directly.
If they text PAUSE, RESUME or SWAP, it is passed to you on Follow-Up.
