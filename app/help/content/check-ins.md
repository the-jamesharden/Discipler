# Check-ins and their rhythm

Once a week, on the day and hour you choose in Settings, Discipler texts each Discipler to ask how their week went.
They answer with a single letter or number, and their answers fill in Check-Ins, Overview and Follow-Up.
Disciples are never asked; only the person leading answers.

## One conversation a week

A Discipler who leads several relationships gets one conversation, not several.
Discipler asks about each relationship in turn, the oldest first, and moves to the next as they reply.
The texts read like this:

> {{exampleMeetingQuestion}}

If they met, Discipler asks how it went:

> {{exampleSatisfactionQuestion}}

If they answer D, for a concern, Discipler asks for a few words, which only you and your other Admins can read:

> {{exampleConcernRequest}}

If they didn't meet, there is nothing more to ask about that relationship, and Discipler moves on.
After the last relationship, it thanks them:

> {{exampleThankYou}}

## Replies people really send

People don't always reply with just a number or letter.
Discipler understands common replies like yes, y, yeah, no, nope, great, good, ok and concern, with or without a thank-you around them.
It never guesses from a sentence: a reply like "it wasn't great" is not read as great.

When a reply can't be read, Discipler says which replies it is looking for, at most {{clarifications}} for each question.
After that it stops asking but keeps listening, so a good reply still counts.

## When nobody answers

If a question goes unanswered, Discipler sends it again after {{reminderAfter}}.
If that goes unanswered too, it moves on to the next relationship, so one quiet week doesn't hold up the rest.
Discipler never starts a text at night: a reminder that falls due at night waits until {{quietEarliest}}.

## What the answers mean

Each relationship's state comes from its answers:

- **Healthy** is the usual state.
- **Stalled** means no answer for {{stalledSilent}} in a row, or "we didn't meet" for {{stalledNotMeeting}} in a row.
- **Needs care** means a concern was raised this week.
- **Paused** means its Discipler paused check-ins for a while.
- **Awaiting acceptance** means its Discipler hasn't accepted it yet, so no check-ins are sent.

A stalled relationship becomes healthy again once its Discipler answers that they met.
A concern stays on [Follow-Up](/help/follow-up) until you resolve it.

## The Check-Ins tab

Check-Ins shows this week: how many check-ins are done, how many are still waiting, and how many concerns are open.
Below, every answer sits in one of four columns, A, B, C and D, by name and date.
Press a relationship to open its card and see this week's answer and when it came in.
A concern's words are never on the list, only on the relationship's card.

## Changing the day or hour

Change the check-in day and hour in [Settings](/help/settings).
The change starts with the next check-in; one already on its way goes out as it was.
