# Refer a church

Know a church that would love Discipler?
Give a month free, and get a month free.

## How it works

1. Open **Refer a church** at the top of any page and press **Copy**.
   It copies a short note with your church's link, ready to paste into a text or an email.
2. The church you invite signs up through your link.
   Their first paid month is free.
3. When they pay their first bill, your church gets a month free too, applied to your bill automatically.

Your month free waits {{referralCreditHeld}} after their first payment, and is taken back if that payment is refunded.
It shows on Billing when it is applied.

## A few things to know

- Discipler sends no invitations for you.
  You share your link however you like.
- We never tell you which churches signed up with your link, and we never tell them it was you, unless you tell each other.
- **Get a new link** replaces your link straight away.
  The old one then opens sign-up at the normal price, with no month free for anyone.
- If both churches pay with the same card or bank account, or share an email domain, the team reviews the credit before it is applied.
