# Signing in and your password

How to get into Discipler, what to do when a password is forgotten, and who has an account at all.

## Signing in

Sign in with your email or your phone number, and your password, in the one box on the sign-in page.
You stay signed in on your own devices for a long time, so you won't often need to.

## Forgot your password?

If you signed up with an email, press **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page.
We email you a link to choose a new password, and it works for {{passwordResetLink}}.

A Discipler who forgets theirs asks an Admin, who resets it from that person's page on the Roster.
The Admin is shown the new password once, to read out or write down; it is never texted or emailed.

## Choosing a password

A password needs at least {{shortestPassword}} characters, and can be as long as you like.
Very common passwords are refused, so pick something only you would know.

Change yours any time with **Change your password** in your account menu.
Changing it signs you out everywhere, on every device, which is useful if someone else may have it.

## Who has an account

Admins and Disciplers have accounts.
Disciples don't: they take part entirely by text.
An account belongs to one church, so there is nothing to switch between.
