# Ask Discipler

Ask Discipler is the round green button with a smiling speech bubble, in the bottom-right corner of your pages.
Ask it anything about how Discipler works, and it answers from these help pages.

## What it can and can't do

It answers from the Help center and nothing else, and each answer ends with a link to the page it came from.
It can't see anyone in your church, so it can't answer about a particular person or relationship.
For that, open the relationship's card, or their row on Check-Ins.

Please don't paste names, phone numbers or prayer requests into it.

## A few limits

- Each Admin can ask {{aiQuestionsPerDay}} questions a day.
  It tells you when you are running low.
- A chat carries on while you stay signed in, and a new sign-in starts a fresh one.
- Your chats are kept for {{aiChatKept}} and then deleted.
  Only you can read them.

## Tucking it away

Press the small minus on its corner to tuck it into a slim tab on the right edge.
It stays tucked on every page until you press the tab.
