# Your test check-in

Before your Disciplers get their first check-in, send one to yourself.
It is the same check-in they get, in your church's own words, texted to your own phone, so you know exactly what they will see.

## Sending one

Press Send test on the test check-in card in your welcome window, or on step three of the Jumpstart discipleship bar.
The texts go only to the phone on your own account, never to anyone else.

The first time, we check that the phone is yours.
We text you a {{verifyCodeDigits}}-digit code, you type it in, and your test goes out.
You are not asked again for that phone.

## Answering it

Answer it on your phone the way a Discipler would.
Discipler asks whether you met, then how it went, just as it asks your Disciplers, and thanks you at the end.

Nothing from a test is counted.
It shows on Check-Ins with a Test tag, and it never changes your Overview, your Follow-Up or any number.

## A few limits

- Test check-ins can only text US numbers for now. If the phone on your account is outside the US, this step is done for you.
- You can send up to {{testCheckInsPerDay}} test check-ins a day.
- A test you leave unanswered ends after {{testCheckInOpen}}.
- If a real check-in is waiting on your phone, answer that first and then try the test again.
- If a real check-in is about to reach your phone while a test is open, the test ends first, so your real answer always lands on the real check-in.

If something isn't working, write to [support@trydiscipler.com](mailto:support@trydiscipler.com) and we will help.
