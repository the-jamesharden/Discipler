# Settings

Settings is where your church's own choices live.
Open it with the gear at the top of any page.
Everything on it is saved together with one Save settings button.

## Billing

The first card shows your plan, your trial or your next payment, and your card, with **Open billing**.
See [Billing and your free trial](/help/billing).

## Ministry

- **Ministry name** is what your church is called on your own screens.
- **Messages read as** is the name at the start of every text, followed by "via Discipler".
  Leave it blank to use the ministry name.
  A church where one person runs discipleship can use that person's name.
- **Timezone** is your church's clock.
  The check-in hour, the hours people are free, the week your check-ins count in, and the month for the rates line are all read against it, so it matters more than it looks.

## Language

Choose what your church calls the person leading and the person being discipled, such as mentor and mentee.
Those words go into your texts exactly as you write them, and a preview shows a real text with your words in it.
Your own screens always say Discipler and Disciple.

## Pairing

- **Gender.** Match gender in one-to-ones, on unless you turn it off.
  While it is on it applies everywhere, including when you pair by hand.
  Groups are whatever they were declared as.
- **Age.** How many age bands a Disciple may be above their Discipler in a suggestion.
  The bands are {{ageBands}}.
  There is no limit the other way, and you can always pair across it by hand.

## Check-in

Choose the day and hour your Disciplers are asked how their week went.
The hour is between {{quietEarliest}} and {{quietLatest}}, on the hour, in your timezone.
A change starts with the next check-in; one already on its way goes out as it was.
Pressing Save here also ticks the first step of Jumpstart discipleship, whether you changed anything or not.

## What the check-in says

Under the day and hour, each sentence of the check-in is a line you can rewrite in your own words.
Write \[name\] where the person's name goes.
Each sentence can be up to {{checkInSentenceLongest}} characters, and a blank line uses Discipler's words.
A phone beside it shows exactly how the text will read.

The start of each text, the reply choices and the rates line stay as Discipler writes them, so replies keep working and the texts stay within the carriers' rules.
Your [test check-in](/help/test-check-in) uses your words too.

## Elsewhere

Your Intake forms, their links and QR codes, and your list of discipleship goals are on **Intake forms**, in your account menu.
See [The Roster and Intake](/help/roster-and-intake).
