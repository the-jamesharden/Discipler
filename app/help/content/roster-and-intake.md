# The Roster and Intake

The Roster is everyone in your ministry, each person once, with every pairing they are in on their row.
Intake is the short form each person fills in themselves before anyone can pair them or text them.

## Adding people to the Roster

There are two ways people arrive on your Roster:

- **You paste them in.** Press Import Dataset on the Roster and paste rows straight from a spreadsheet.
  If your rows already say who disciples whom, choose Already paired; otherwise choose People only.
  Discipler shows you what it will add before anything happens.
- **They fill in your Intake form.** Anyone who completes it lands on the Roster by themselves.

Not sure your spreadsheet will come out right?
Email it to [support@trydiscipler.com](mailto:support@trydiscipler.com) and we will import it for you.

For its first {{newChurchFirstDays}}, a new church can have up to {{newChurchPeople}} people on its Roster and send up to {{newChurchTextsPerWeek}} texts a week.
An import that would go past that is refused, and texts past it wait until there is room.
If your church is larger, email the same address and we will lift both.

## Why Intake matters

Being on the Roster is not the same as agreeing to be texted.
Somebody you paste in is marked Awaiting Intake, and Discipler will not text them or let you pair them until they complete Intake themselves.
On the form they agree, in their own words, to receive texts and to share their name and number with the people they are paired with.

The moment someone completes Intake, they are sent a short welcome text, and they are ready to pair.

## Your Intake forms

Your church has two forms, each with its own link and QR code:

- **The mentor and mentee form**, for people who want to disciple someone or be discipled one to one.
- **The groups form**, for people joining a group.

Find both under Intake forms in your account menu.
The links are short enough to print in a bulletin or say from the front, and every link your church has ever used keeps working, even after you change it.

Each form asks for a name, a mobile number, gender, an age band, a discipleship goal, and the hours they are free, from {{gridFirstHour}} to {{gridLastHour}} on each day of the week.
Email is optional.
On Intake forms you can reword any question, add questions of your own, and take out the ones you don't need.
The page warns you before you remove a question that pairing relies on.

## A person's own page

Press a name on the Roster to open their page.
From there you can:

- Make an **Intake link** just for them.
  It opens their own form, with their answers already in it, so they can update when they are free.
  It works for {{intakeLinkLifetime}}, and you send it to them yourself.
- **Reset the password** of a Discipler who has an account.
  You are shown the new password once, to pass on.
- **Unpair** them from a pairing.
- **Remove them from the Roster.** This ends their pairings and keeps their history.

## Opted out

Somebody who texts STOP is marked Opted out on the Roster.
Discipler stops texting them, and they cannot be paired until they text START.
See [Texts, STOP, START and HELP](/help/texting).
