# Pairing people

A pairing puts a Disciple with a Discipler.
You can take a suggestion from Suggested Pairs, or pair anyone yourself from the Roster.
Only people who have completed Intake can be paired, on either side.

## Suggested Pairs

Suggested Pairs offers one-to-one matches, strongest first, each with a plain sentence saying why.
Your church's rules come first, and anyone they rule out is never suggested:

- **Gender.** One-to-ones are men with men and women with women, unless you turn this off in Settings.
- **Age.** A Discipler is not suggested for a Disciple who is too many age bands older than they are.
  You choose how many bands in Settings; a new church starts at {{defaultAgeGap}}.

Then matches are ranked by the hours both people said they are free:

- **Excellent fit** shares at least {{excellentHours}} hours, across at least {{excellentDays}} different days.
- **Good fit** shares at least {{goodHours}} hours.
- **Recommended** shares a single hour.

A shared discipleship goal moves a match up among others like it.
People who share no free hours with anyone are listed at the bottom under No Schedule Overlap, so nobody is forgotten, and you can still pair them by hand.

## Pairing by hand

Press **Pair** beside a name on the Roster.
Say which side of this pairing they are on, Disciples somebody or Is discipled, and choose the other person.
Tick more than one person to make:

- a **1:2 pair**, one Discipler with two Disciples together,
- separate **1:1 pairs**, one for each person you ticked, or
- a **Group**, with a name, for men, women or both.

To put somebody into a group that already exists, choose the group and press Add to group, or Add as co-leader for a second Discipler.
The gender rule applies when you pair by hand too, while it is turned on.

## After you pair them

A new relationship waits for its Discipler to accept it.
Discipler texts the Discipler a link that shows who they have been paired with.
They choose a password and press Accept.

- Nothing is sent to the Disciple until the Discipler accepts.
- If the Discipler hasn't accepted after {{acceptanceReminder}}, Discipler reminds them once.
- After {{acceptanceEscalation}}, it shows on [Follow-Up](/help/follow-up) so you can check in with them.
- The link works for {{invitationLifetime}}.
  Follow-Up offers a new one if it runs out.

When the Discipler accepts, everyone in the relationship is texted that it has started, and the weekly [check-ins](/help/check-ins) begin.

## Groups and the groups form

People who fill in your groups form choose a group themselves.
You can set a group to ask first, and then each request waits on Intake forms for you to admit them.
Somebody who has no group in mind waits on Follow-Up for you to place them.

## Ending a pairing

Press **Unpair** beside a pairing on a person's page, or End relationship on its card.
You are asked how it ended and why, and the history is kept.
Everyone in it can be paired again.
