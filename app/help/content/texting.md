# Texts, STOP, START and HELP

Discipler's texts are how your Disciplers and Disciples hear from your church.
Here is how they are sent, and the words people can text back.

## Who the texts come from

Discipler sends from a shared set of Discipler phone numbers.
Each person always hears from the same number for your church, so their thread stays in one place.
Somebody who belongs to two churches on Discipler hears from a different number for each, so their replies never get mixed up.

Every text starts with your church's name followed by "via Discipler", for example "{{exampleChurch}} via Discipler:".
The name is the one in Messages read as, in [Settings](/help/settings).

Discipler writes the frame of every text: that start, the reply choices, and the line about rates and opting out.
You can reword the check-in's own sentences in Settings, inside that frame.

## Quiet hours

Discipler only starts a conversation between {{quietEarliest}} and {{quietLatest}}, in your church's timezone.
A reminder that would fall at night waits for the morning.
When somebody texts in, they are answered straight away, whatever the hour.

## The rates line

Texts sometimes end with "Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help."
Each person gets this line at most once a calendar month, on the first text of the month that can carry it, so it doesn't crowd every message.
Their very first text and every reply to HELP always carry it.

## Words anyone can text

- **STOP** stops every text from your church to that person.
  They are marked Opted out on the Roster, and their relationships stay as they are.
  A STOP to one church doesn't stop another church's texts.
- **START** lets your church text them again after a STOP.
  It doesn't restart anything else.
- **HELP** replies with the words they can use and asks them to contact your church for anything else.

## Words for Disciplers

- **PAUSE** pauses check-ins for one relationship, for {{pausePeriods}} weeks.
  Discipler asks how long, and {{defaultPause}} is the default.
- **RESUME** starts a paused relationship again.
- **SWAP** asks you to pair them with somebody else.

A Discipler with more than one relationship is asked which one they mean.
If they don't finish the exchange within {{keywordExchangeOpen}}, it lapses and nothing changes.

## What Discipler never does

Nothing you press in Discipler sends a text you write yourself, and there is no inbox.
When somebody needs a word from you, Discipler shows you who to call, and you pick up the phone.
