# Materials

Materials are what your relationships work through together: a book, a study, a plan.
The Materials tab keeps your church's own list, and shows which relationships are on each one.

## Adding a material

Press **New material** and give it a title.
Add some text, files, links, or any mix of them, up to {{materialItems}} files and links in all.
A file can be a document, an image, audio or video, up to {{largestFile}} each.

To change a material, press Edit on its folder.
You can rename it, change its text, and add or remove files and links.

## Assigning one

Each material is a folder holding the relationships working through it now.
A last, dashed folder holds the relationships on no material.

- On any relationship's card in a folder, choose a material and save to move it on.
- Press **Assign to more** on a folder to start several relationships on it at once.
- A group's material can also be set on its card on Intake forms.

A relationship works through one material at a time.
When it moves to a new one, the old one is closed with its dates, so you can always see what a relationship worked through and when.

The filter at the top shows All, Men's or Women's relationships.

## What people are sent

When the material a relationship is working through changes, the people in it are texted once to say so, and each Disciple gets a link to open it.
Discipler waits until your changes have been still for {{materialTextWait}}, sends each person at most one such text a day, and sends nothing if things end up as they were.
The button itself sends nothing.

## Removing a material

Press Edit, then Remove, and press again to confirm.
A material can't be removed while any relationship is working through it.
Once removed it leaves the tab and every list, and the weeks relationships spent on it still say so.
