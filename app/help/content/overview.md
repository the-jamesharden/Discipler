# Overview

Overview answers one question at a glance: how is your ministry doing right now?
It is the first tab, and where you land when you sign in.

## The numbers across the top

- **Active Relationships** counts pairs and groups that are running.
  Paused ones are listed but not counted.
- **Meeting Rate** is how many of last week's answers said they met.
- **Response Rate** is how many of last week's check-ins were answered.
- **This Week** is how many check-ins have been completed so far this week.
- **Needs Follow-Up** is how many things are waiting for you on [Follow-Up](/help/follow-up).

Meeting Rate and Response Rate measure different things on purpose.
A ministry can answer every week and still not be meeting, and that is exactly what you want to see.

## Last week

The two rates, both charts and Quick Stats cover last week, the most recent week whose check-ins have all finished.
That way the numbers hold still instead of starting at zero every Monday and climbing.
A week's check-ins finish when every one is answered, or {{givenUpAfter}} after they went out.

**Meeting Completion** shows meetings held against meetings missed.
**Check-In Ratings** shows how the meetings went: A outstanding, B good, C okay and D concern.
**Quick Stats** repeats the headline figures, with the quality rate: A and B answers out of every rated answer.

## Growth in discipleship

The green line shows how many people are being discipled over time.
Choose Total discipled, People in mentorship or People in groups, over the last {{weeksOnTheChart}} weeks or every month since your church began.
Each person counts once, and only while they are being discipled; leading doesn't count.

## Your relationships

Below the charts are your relationships and groups, each card coloured by what it needs:

- **Green** is healthy.
- **Yellow** is stalled.
- **Red** carries a concern you haven't resolved yet.
- **Grey** is paused, or waiting for its Discipler to accept.

Every colour comes with words on the card, so nothing relies on colour alone.
Press a card to see who is in it, how to reach them, their last answer, and anything that needs you.
Opening a card sends nothing.
