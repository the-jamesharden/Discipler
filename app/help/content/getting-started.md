# Getting started

Discipler helps your church keep its discipleship relationships meeting, week after week.
You add your people, pair each Disciple with a Discipler, and Discipler texts each Discipler once a week to ask how it is going.
When something needs you, it shows up on Follow-Up.

## Signing up your church

Anyone can start their church on Discipler from the sign-up page.
It takes a few minutes:

1. A quick check that you are a person and not a bot.
2. Your email and your mobile number.
3. A {{emailCodeDigits}}-digit code we email you, to confirm your email.
   It works for {{emailCodeLifetime}}.
4. Your church's name, your name, a password and your timezone.
5. A card or bank account, through Stripe.

You are not charged when you sign up.
Your free trial lasts {{trialDays}}, and the first charge comes when it ends.
See [Billing and your free trial](/help/billing) for prices and offers.

When you finish, you land on your Overview, signed in, with your welcome open.

## Jumpstart discipleship

Under the tabs sits a slim bar called Jumpstart discipleship.
It counts {{setupSteps}} steps and ticks each one off by itself as it happens, so there is nothing to tick:

1. Confirm your check-in day and hour in Settings, by pressing Save.
2. Take the tour.
3. Send yourself a [test check-in](/help/test-check-in).
4. Add people to your [Roster](/help/roster-and-intake).
5. Receive your first Intake.
6. Create your first relationship, by [pairing](/help/pairing) two people.
7. See that relationship accepted by its Discipler.

The bar shows what to do next, with a button for it.
Every Admin of your church shares the same bar, and it never goes backwards.
When all of the steps are done, the bar goes away.

## Your welcome window

The first time you sign in, a welcome window opens with three cards: a short tour of Discipler, your test check-in, and a way to speak with the team directly at office hours.
You can close it at any time and bring it back from the Help menu with Restart onboarding.

The tour is a demo of a made-up church, Example Ministry, so nothing in it is yours.
Press Next to move through it, and Done at the end.

## Where things are

- **Overview** is how your ministry is doing this week.
  See [Overview](/help/overview).
- **Check-Ins** shows this week's answers.
  See [Check-ins](/help/check-ins).
- **Suggested Pairs** offers good matches.
  See [Pairing people](/help/pairing).
- **Follow-Up** lists everything that needs you.
  See [Follow-Up](/help/follow-up).
- **Materials** is what your relationships are working through.
  See [Materials](/help/materials).
- **Roster** is everyone in your ministry.
  See [The Roster and Intake](/help/roster-and-intake).

The gear at the top opens [Settings](/help/settings), the question mark opens Help, and your initials open your account.
